Welcome to Bucknell’s GIS Certification Program

Our goal is to prepare undergraduate students at Bucknell to take and pass the Pre-GISP (or GISP-E) exam offered by the GIS Certification Institute. This website contains information on a variety of pathways for Bucknell undergraduate students to follow to gain the knowledge and skills necessary to pass the GISP-E exam.

These pathways are meant to be 'mix and match'—meaning that each student can pick and choose from different pathways to piece together the knowledge and skills they need to pass the exam and earn the GISP-E certification.
