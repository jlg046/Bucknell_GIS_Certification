Pathway 3: Web-Based, Self-Paced Training Courses

Utilize online learning frameworks to bolster your alignment with the 11 core blueprint competencies.

Esri Web-Based Training Courses ArcGIS Ecosystem

Targeted Learning Pathways: Curated self-paced courses from the Esri Academy that align with specific blueprint categories (such as coordinate systems, cartography, or relational database design).

  • Goal: Leverage free or institutional-license Bucknell Esri accounts to build fundamental technical skills in a structured workspace.
  • Strategy: Complete targeted modules that provide official certificates of completion to attach to your portfolio logs.

Non-Esri Web-Based Training Courses Vendor-Neutral

Platform-Agnostic Mastery: Because the GISP-E examination is strictly vendor-neutral, students must study open-access materials, QGIS core documentation tutorials, spatial data science MOOCs, and open textbook chapters.

  • Goal: Ensure students understand deep theoretical concepts (e.g., spatial overlay joins, raster cell algorithms, topological rules) rather than just memorizing "which software button to click."
  • Primary Text Resource: Cross-reference concepts with the official Geographic Information Science & Technology Body of Knowledge (UCGIS BoK).
